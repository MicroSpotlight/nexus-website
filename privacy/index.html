Privacy隐私

Privacy Policy隐私政策

Effective August 2, 2026Applies to Nexus for iOS and iPadOS.适用于 iOS 与 iPadOS 版 Nexus。
Nexus does not provide a Nexus account and does not operate a media catalog. Most library data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores locally, what optional services receive, and the controls available in Privacy & Data.Nexus 不提供 Nexus 账号，也不运营媒体片库。大多数片库数据保留在你的设备上。本政策说明 App 在本地保存的内容、可选服务接收的内容，以及“隐私与数据”中的控制项。

1. Who we are1. 我们是谁

Nexus is developed and published by MicroSpotlight ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the Nexus iOS/iPadOS application and this website. The support portal is the public contact route for questions about this policy or personal data.Nexus 由 MicroSpotlight（“我们”）开发和发布。本政策适用于 Nexus iOS/iPadOS App 及本网站。如对本政策或个人数据有疑问，请通过技术支持页面联系。

Open the support portal →打开技术支持页面 →

2. Data stored on your device2. 保存在设备上的数据

Nexus stores the following locally to provide the features you request:为了提供你主动使用的功能，Nexus 可能在设备本地保存以下数据：

  • Media source names, endpoint settings, grouping, sorting, and source health summaries.媒体来源名称、接口设置、分组、排序和来源健康摘要。
  • Playback progress, viewing history, favorites, library preferences, language, appearance, playback settings, and download settings.播放进度、观看记录、收藏、片库偏好、语言、外观、播放设置和下载设置。
  • Downloaded media files and user-authorized local file or folder bookmarks.下载的媒体文件，以及用户授权的本地文件或文件夹书签。
  • Optional metadata matches and minimized diagnostic summaries.可选的元数据匹配结果和最小化诊断摘要。

Passwords and other source credentials are stored in Apple Keychain references, not in ordinary source exports. Nexus does not upload your source settings, source addresses, local paths, credentials, cookies, or tokens to a MicroSpotlight-owned server.密码和其他来源凭据通过 Apple 钥匙串引用保存，不会写入普通来源导出文件。Nexus 不会把来源设置、来源地址、本地路径、凭据、Cookie 或 Token 上传到 MicroSpotlight 自有服务器。

3. Requests to sources you add3. 对你添加的来源发起请求

When you add a local file, network share, WebDAV server, media server, or compatible catalog endpoint, Nexus communicates with that service from your device. The service may receive the requests, credentials, IP address, and other information required by its own configuration. That service is not operated by MicroSpotlight, and its privacy policy applies to its processing.当你添加本地文件、网络共享、WebDAV 服务、媒体服务器或兼容内容接口时，Nexus 会从你的设备向该服务通信。该服务可能根据自身配置接收请求、凭据、IP 地址及连接所需的其他信息。该服务不由 MicroSpotlight 运营，其数据处理适用该服务自己的隐私政策。

Only add sources and download media that you are authorized to access and save. Nexus does not bypass DRM, encrypted HLS, authentication, paywalls, geographic restrictions, or other access controls.请只添加你有权访问的来源，并只下载你有权保存的媒体。Nexus 不绕过 DRM、加密 HLS、登录鉴权、付费墙、地域限制或其他访问控制。

4. Optional Apple services4. 可选的 Apple 服务

CloudKit syncCloudKit 同步

CloudKit sync is off by default and is enabled only when you choose it in Privacy & Data. If enabled, Nexus stores minimized library and playback-preference snapshots in your private CloudKit database. Snapshots can include opaque source IDs, hashed asset identities, minimal titles or episode labels, playback positions, timestamps, and selected playback preferences. They exclude source addresses and settings, playback/subtitle/poster URLs, headers, cookies, account names, passwords, tokens, Keychain references, diagnostics settings, language, appearance, viewing history, and downloads. You can delete Nexus CloudKit snapshots from the app without deleting your local library.CloudKit 同步默认关闭，只有你在“隐私与数据”中主动开启后才会使用。开启后，Nexus 会把最小化的片库与播放偏好快照写入你的私有 CloudKit 数据库。快照可能包含不透明来源 ID、哈希后的媒体身份、最小化标题或集数标签、播放位置、时间戳和部分播放偏好；不包含来源地址与设置、播放/字幕/海报 URL、请求头、Cookie、账号名、密码、Token、钥匙串引用、诊断设置、语言、外观、观看记录或下载文件。你可以在 App 内删除 Nexus 的 CloudKit 快照，而不删除本地片库。

System integrations系统集成

Widgets, Live Activities, Handoff, and supported system launchers are optional. They use minimized local snapshots and opaque identifiers. They are designed not to expose source settings, source addresses, media URLs, credentials, cookies, or raw errors on system surfaces.小组件、实时活动、接力和支持的系统启动器均为可选功能。它们使用最小化的本地快照和不透明标识符，设计上不会在系统界面暴露来源设置、来源地址、媒体 URL、凭据、Cookie 或原始错误。

5. Optional metadata and diagnostics5. 可选元数据与诊断

TMDB metadata matchingTMDB 元数据匹配

If you provide your own TMDB API Read Access Token, it is stored in Keychain. When you manually search or confirm a match, Nexus sends the title or the TMDB IDs and season/episode numbers needed to retrieve public metadata. It does not send your original file path, SMB/WebDAV address, source credentials, or account password to TMDB. TMDB is a third-party service and its own terms and privacy policy apply.如果你提供自己的 TMDB API Read Access Token，该 Token 会保存在钥匙串中。手动搜索或确认匹配时，Nexus 只发送获取公开元数据所需的标题，或 TMDB ID 与季/集编号；不会把原始文件路径、SMB/WebDAV 地址、来源凭据或账号密码发送给 TMDB。TMDB 是第三方服务，适用其自身条款和隐私政策。

Firebase Analytics and CrashlyticsFirebase Analytics 与 Crashlytics

The current build may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for app-launch analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, performance signals, app/device version information, and identifiers required by the SDK configuration. Collection is controlled from Privacy & Data and the final App Store privacy labels must match the shipped configuration. We do not use Nexus library titles, source addresses, credentials, media URLs, or raw errors as analytics payloads.当前构建可能使用 Firebase Analytics 与 Crashlytics 处理启动分析、诊断、崩溃报告、性能信号、App/设备版本信息，以及 SDK 配置所需的标识符。数据收集可在“隐私与数据”中控制，最终 App Store 隐私标签应与实际提交构建一致。我们不会把 Nexus 片库标题、来源地址、凭据、媒体 URL 或原始错误作为分析负载。

You can turn off the available analytics and diagnostics choices in Privacy & Data. Disabling collection does not delete local library data.你可以在“隐私与数据”中关闭可用的分析与诊断选项。关闭收集不会删除本地片库数据。

6. Sharing, advertising, and retention6. 共享、广告与保留期限

We do not sell your personal information and Nexus does not include third-party advertising. Data may be processed by Apple, Firebase, TMDB, or a source provider only when the related feature is used or configured. Local records remain until you clear them, remove the source, delete the app, or use the relevant data controls. Redacted diagnostic histories are bounded and can be cleared independently. CloudKit snapshots remain until you disable sync or delete them from Nexus, subject to Apple's service operation and retention.我们不出售个人信息，Nexus 也不包含第三方广告。只有在你使用或配置相关功能时，数据才可能由 Apple、Firebase、TMDB 或来源服务商处理。本地记录会保留到你清除记录、移除来源、删除 App 或使用相关数据控制项为止。脱敏诊断历史有数量与时间上限，可单独清除。CloudKit 快照会保留到你关闭同步或在 Nexus 中删除为止，同时受 Apple 服务运行和保留机制影响。

7. Your choices7. 你的选择

  • Use Privacy & Data to review local data categories, disable optional collection, and manage integrations.通过“隐私与数据”查看本地数据类别、关闭可选收集并管理系统集成。
  • Clear favorites, playback progress, history, diagnostics, downloads, or CloudKit snapshots using the separate controls provided by Nexus.使用 Nexus 提供的独立控制项清除收藏、播放进度、记录、诊断、下载或 CloudKit 快照。
  • Remove a folder authorization from Files access; this removes Nexus access and does not modify files in that folder.移除 Files 文件夹授权；这只会移除 Nexus 的访问权，不会修改文件夹中的文件。
  • For a privacy question, contact us through the support portal. Do not post credentials, private source URLs, tokens, or personal data in a public issue.如有隐私问题，请通过技术支持页面联系。请不要在公开 Issue 中发布凭据、私有来源 URL、Token 或个人数据。

8. Children8. 儿童隐私

Nexus does not provide accounts, behavioral profiles, or a social publishing service. It is a general-purpose player for user-selected media sources. Parents and guardians are responsible for the sources and content added to a device.Nexus 不提供账号、行为画像或社交发布服务，是面向用户选择的媒体来源的通用播放器。家长或监护人应对设备中添加的来源和内容负责。

9. Changes and contact9. 政策变更与联系

We may update this policy when Nexus adds or changes data practices. The effective date at the top will change with a material revision. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to the relevant use of Nexus.当 Nexus 增加或调整数据处理方式时，我们可能更新本政策。重大修改会同步更新顶部生效日期。更新后继续使用 Nexus，表示更新后的政策适用于相关使用行为。
