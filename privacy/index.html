Privacy隐私

Privacy Policy隐私政策

Effective August 15, 2026Applies to Nexus for iOS, iPadOS, Android, and this website.适用于 iOS、iPadOS、Android 版 Nexus 及本网站。
Nexus does not provide a Nexus account and does not operate a media catalog. Most library data stays on your device. This policy explains what each platform stores locally, what optional platform services receive, and the controls available to you.Nexus 不提供 Nexus 账号，也不运营媒体片库。大多数片库数据保留在你的设备上。本政策说明各平台在本地保存的内容、可选平台服务接收的内容，以及你可使用的控制项。
iPhone + iPad Keychain and private CloudKit钥匙串与私有 CloudKit

Credentials use Apple Keychain. Private CloudKit sync is optional and off by default.凭据使用 Apple 钥匙串；私有 CloudKit 同步为可选功能，默认关闭。

Android Android Keystore and encrypted backupAndroid Keystore 与加密备份

Credentials are encrypted with a Keystore-held key. Eligible minimized data may use Android backup or device transfer.凭据由 Keystore 中的密钥加密；符合条件的最小化数据可使用 Android 备份或设备迁移。

The support portal is the public contact route for questions about this policy or personal data.Nexus 由 MicroSpotlight（“我们”）开发和发布。本政策适用于 Nexus iOS/iPadOS、Android App 及本网站。如对本政策或个人数据有疑问，请通过技术支持页面联系。

2. Data stored on your device2. 保存在设备上的数据

Nexus stores the following locally to provide the features you request:为了提供你主动使用的功能，Nexus 可能在设备本地保存以下数据：

  • Media source names, endpoint settings, grouping, sorting, and source health summaries.媒体来源名称、接口设置、分组、排序和来源健康摘要。
  • Playback progress, viewing history, favorites, watch later, custom lists, smart rules, library preferences, search history, language, appearance, playback settings, and download settings.播放进度、观看记录、收藏、稍后观看、自定义列表、智能规则、片库偏好、搜索记录、语言、外观、播放设置和下载设置。
  • Downloaded media files and user-authorized local file or folder access.下载的媒体文件，以及用户授权的本地文件或文件夹访问权。
  • Optional metadata matches and bounded, redacted diagnostic summaries.可选的元数据匹配结果，以及有保留上限且经过脱敏的诊断摘要。

On iPhone and iPad, passwords and source credentials use Apple Keychain references. On Android, credentials and media-server sessions are encrypted with AES-GCM using keys held by Android Keystore. Ordinary source exports exclude credentials. Nexus does not upload source settings, source addresses, local paths, credentials, cookies, or tokens to a MicroSpotlight-owned server.在 iPhone 与 iPad 上，密码和来源凭据通过 Apple 钥匙串引用保存；在 Android 上，凭据与媒体服务器会话使用 Android Keystore 中的密钥进行 AES-GCM 加密。普通来源导出不包含凭据。Nexus 不会把来源设置、来源地址、本地路径、凭据、Cookie 或 Token 上传到 MicroSpotlight 自有服务器。

3. Requests to sources you add3. 对你添加的来源发起请求

When you add a local file, network share, WebDAV server, media server, or compatible catalog endpoint, Nexus communicates with that service from your device. The service may receive the requests, credentials, IP address, device network information, and other information required by its own configuration. That service is not operated by MicroSpotlight, and its privacy policy applies to its processing.当你添加本地文件、网络共享、WebDAV 服务、媒体服务器或兼容内容接口时，Nexus 会从你的设备向该服务通信。该服务可能根据自身配置接收请求、凭据、IP 地址、设备网络信息及连接所需的其他信息。该服务不由 MicroSpotlight 运营，其数据处理适用该服务自己的隐私政策。

Only add sources and download media that you are authorized to access and save. Nexus does not bypass DRM, encrypted HLS, authentication, paywalls, geographic restrictions, or other access controls.请只添加你有权访问的来源，并只下载你有权保存的媒体。Nexus 不绕过 DRM、加密 HLS、登录鉴权、付费墙、地域限制或其他访问控制。

4. Optional platform cloud and system services4. 可选的平台云服务与系统服务

Apple CloudKit syncApple CloudKit 同步

On iPhone and iPad, CloudKit sync is off by default and is enabled only when you choose it in Privacy & Data. If enabled, Nexus stores minimized library and playback-preference snapshots in your private CloudKit database. Snapshots can include opaque source IDs, hashed asset identities, minimal titles or episode labels, playback positions, timestamps, lists, rules, and selected preferences. They exclude source addresses and settings, playback/subtitle/poster URLs, headers, cookies, account names, passwords, tokens, Keychain references, diagnostics settings, and downloaded files. You can delete Nexus CloudKit snapshots from the app without deleting your local library.在 iPhone 与 iPad 上，CloudKit 同步默认关闭，只有你在“隐私与数据”中主动开启后才会使用。开启后，Nexus 会把最小化的片库与播放偏好快照写入你的私有 CloudKit 数据库。快照可能包含不透明来源 ID、哈希后的媒体身份、最小化标题或集数标签、播放位置、时间戳、列表、规则和部分偏好；不包含来源地址与设置、播放/字幕/海报 URL、请求头、Cookie、账号名、密码、Token、钥匙串引用、诊断设置或下载文件。你可以在 App 内删除 Nexus 的 CloudKit 快照，而不删除本地片库。

Android backup and device transferAndroid 备份与设备迁移

On Android, selected app preferences, smart rules, and an opt-in source-free sync snapshot may be eligible for Android's encrypted cloud backup or device-to-device transfer when those services are enabled on your device. The backup rules exclude source addresses and settings, playback URLs, local paths, downloaded media, download tasks, source credentials, and media-server session tokens. Backup transport, account association, retention, and deletion are controlled by Android and the backup provider configured on your device—not by MicroSpotlight. Deleting the Nexus sync snapshot stops future snapshot updates; operating-system backup copies are managed in your Android backup settings.在 Android 上，当设备启用相关服务时，部分 App 偏好、智能规则和由你主动开启的无来源信息同步快照可能符合 Android 加密云备份或设备间迁移条件。备份规则排除来源地址与设置、播放 URL、本地路径、下载媒体、下载任务、来源凭据和媒体服务器会话 Token。备份传输、账号关联、保留与删除由 Android 及设备中配置的备份服务商控制，而不是 MicroSpotlight。删除 Nexus 同步快照会停止后续快照更新；操作系统中的备份副本需在 Android 备份设置中管理。

System integrations系统集成

Widgets, Live Activities, Handoff, Android app shortcuts, media controls, notifications, picture in picture, and supported system launchers are optional or activated only when you use the related feature. They use minimized local snapshots and opaque identifiers and are designed not to expose source settings, source addresses, media URLs, credentials, cookies, or raw errors on system surfaces.小组件、实时活动、接力、Android 应用快捷方式、媒体控制、通知、画中画和支持的系统启动器均为可选功能，或仅在你使用相关功能时启用。它们使用最小化的本地快照和不透明标识符，设计上不会在系统界面暴露来源设置、来源地址、媒体 URL、凭据、Cookie 或原始错误。

5. Optional metadata, diagnostics, and website preferences5. 可选元数据、诊断与网站偏好

TMDB metadata matchingTMDB 元数据匹配

If you provide your own TMDB API Read Access Token, it is protected by Apple Keychain or Android Keystore according to your platform. When you manually search or confirm a match, Nexus sends the title or the TMDB IDs and season/episode numbers needed to retrieve public metadata. It does not send your original file path, SMB/WebDAV address, source credentials, or account password to TMDB. TMDB is a third-party service and its own terms and privacy policy apply.如果你提供自己的 TMDB API Read Access Token，该 Token 会根据平台由 Apple 钥匙串或 Android Keystore 保护。手动搜索或确认匹配时，Nexus 只发送获取公开元数据所需的标题，或 TMDB ID 与季/集编号；不会把原始文件路径、SMB/WebDAV 地址、来源凭据或账号密码发送给 TMDB。TMDB 是第三方服务，适用其自身条款和隐私政策。

Diagnostics by platform各平台的诊断方式

The iOS/iPadOS build may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for app-launch analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, performance signals, app/device version information, and identifiers required by the SDK configuration. Available collection choices are controlled from Privacy & Data, and the final App Store privacy labels must match the shipped configuration.iOS/iPadOS 构建可能使用 Firebase Analytics 与 Crashlytics 处理启动分析、诊断、崩溃报告、性能信号、App/设备版本信息，以及 SDK 配置所需的标识符。可用的数据收集选项可在“隐私与数据”中控制，最终 App Store 隐私标签应与实际提交构建一致。

The current Android release does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Android source-health and performance diagnostics are stored locally as bounded, redacted summaries and can be reviewed or cleared in the app. We do not use library titles, source addresses, credentials, media URLs, local paths, record IDs, or raw errors as analytics payloads on either platform.当前 Android 版本不包含 Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics。Android 的来源健康与性能诊断以有保留上限、经过脱敏的摘要形式保存在本地，可在 App 内查看或清除。无论在哪个平台，我们都不会把片库标题、来源地址、凭据、媒体 URL、本地路径、记录 ID 或原始错误作为分析负载。

Google Cast on AndroidAndroid 上的 Google Cast

The Android app includes the Google Cast Sender SDK for device discovery and casting. The SDK automatically collects anonymous App Activity about generic discovery and session-management events, mobile-device information, SDK and feature metadata, and client Cast app information. Portions of this data are sent over an encrypted connection to a Google log collection server and used in aggregate to monitor SDK adoption and performance and detect defects. The logs do not contain identifiers traceable to a specific user, and neither Nexus nor the user can opt out of or delete this SDK data. Nexus does not add library titles, source addresses, credentials, media URLs, local paths, record IDs, or raw errors to these Cast analytics payloads.Android App 包含用于设备发现与投屏的 Google Cast Sender SDK。该 SDK 会自动收集匿名的应用活动，包括通用发现事件、会话管理事件、移动设备信息、SDK 与功能元数据以及客户端 Cast App 信息；其中部分数据会通过加密连接发送到 Google 日志收集服务器，并以聚合形式用于监控 SDK 的采用率与性能及发现缺陷。日志不包含可追溯到特定用户的标识符，Nexus 与用户均无法停用或删除这部分 SDK 数据。Nexus 不会额外把片库标题、来源地址、凭据、媒体 URL、本地路径、记录 ID 或原始错误加入 Cast 分析负载。

Website preferences网站偏好

This website has no analytics tracker, advertising SDK, external font, or tracking cookie. It stores only your language and light/dark theme choices in browser local storage so the site can remember them on your device.本网站不包含分析追踪器、广告 SDK、外部字体或跟踪 Cookie，只会在浏览器本地存储中保存语言与浅色/深色主题选择，以便在你的设备上记住这些偏好。

6. Sharing, advertising, and retention6. 共享、广告与保留期限

We do not sell your personal information, and Nexus does not include third-party advertising. Data may be processed by Apple, Android or your configured backup provider, Google through the Cast SDK on Android, Firebase on Apple platforms when enabled, TMDB, or a source provider only when the related feature is used or configured. Local records remain until you clear them, remove the source, delete the app, or use the relevant data controls. Redacted diagnostic histories are bounded and can be cleared independently. Cloud snapshots or operating-system backups remain subject to the controls, operation, and retention of the platform service that stores them.我们不出售个人信息，Nexus 也不包含第三方广告。只有在你使用或配置相关功能时，数据才可能由 Apple、Android 或你配置的备份服务商、Android 上通过 Cast SDK 提供服务的 Google、Apple 平台上启用的 Firebase、TMDB 或来源服务商处理。本地记录会保留到你清除记录、移除来源、删除 App 或使用相关数据控制项为止。脱敏诊断历史有数量与时间上限，可单独清除。云端快照或操作系统备份还受保存它们的平台服务之控制项、运行方式和保留机制影响。
