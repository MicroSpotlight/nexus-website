02

Browse one library.浏览一个统一片库。

Search across enabled sources, continue watching, save favorites, inspect connection health, and keep history consistent across compatible media.跨已启用来源搜索，继续观看、收藏内容、查看连接健康状态，并为兼容媒体保持一致的观看记录。

03

Play with precision.精确播放每一帧。
