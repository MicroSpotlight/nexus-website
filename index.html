01
On-device by default默认保存在设备上

Sources, playback progress, history, favorites, preferences, and downloads remain local by default.来源、播放进度、记录、收藏、偏好与下载默认保存在本机。

System-protected credentials由系统保护凭据

Apple Keychain and Android Keystore keep credentials outside ordinary source exports and backups.Apple 钥匙串与 Android Keystore 让凭据远离普通来源导出与备份。
