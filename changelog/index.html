0.1.0In preparation准备上架

First App Store release首次 App Store 版本

August 2, 2026iOS / iPadOS 17+
  • Added a unified adaptive library for user-configured catalog endpoints, local files, SMB/NAS, WebDAV, and compatible media servers.新增面向用户自填内容接口、本机文件、SMB/NAS、WebDAV 与兼容媒体服务器的自适应统一片库。
  • Added cross-source search, favorites, viewing history, continue watching, source health, and guided recovery for connection failures.新增跨来源搜索、收藏、观看记录、继续观看、来源健康状态与连接失败恢复指引。
  • Added native/VLC playback selection, picture in picture, background audio, audio-track controls, subtitle delay and styling, and playback fallback handling.新增原生/VLC 播放选择、画中画、后台音频、音轨控制、字幕延迟与样式，以及播放失败回退。
  • Expanded ASS/SSA subtitle rendering with authored positioning, movement, fade, transforms, X/Y/Z rotation, shear, and explicit rotation origin support.扩展 ASS/SSA 字幕渲染，支持作者定位、移动、淡入淡出、变换、X/Y/Z 旋转、剪切与显式旋转原点。
  • Added authorized offline downloads, Wi-Fi and concurrency controls, resilient SMB/WebDAV transfers, and optional download Live Activities.新增经授权的离线下载、Wi‑Fi 与并发控制、可恢复的 SMB/WebDAV 传输及可选下载实时活动。
  • Added local-first privacy controls, a redacted on-device data inventory, independent record deletion, optional private CloudKit snapshots, widgets, and playback Handoff.新增本地优先隐私控制、脱敏设备数据清单、独立记录删除、可选私有 CloudKit 快照、小组件与播放接力。
  • Added Simplified Chinese and English interfaces, configurable appearance and accent colors, alternate app icons, and layouts adapted for iPhone and iPad.新增简体中文与英文界面、外观和主色配置、备选 App 图标，以及针对 iPhone 与 iPad 的自适应布局。
